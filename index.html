How to request

  1. Choose your countries and years above and note the total (€7.50 × the number of country-years; e.g. Austria 2018–2026 is 9 country-years, €67.50).
  2. Transfer the total to the account below, putting your institutional email and the countries and years in the reference, e.g. max.mustermann(at)university.com; Austria 2018-2026, Germany 2020-2026.
  3. Allow up to seven business days after we receive payment. We send the data to the email address in your reference.

Terms of use

Who can use the data

Access is only for non-commercial academic use by students and researchers affiliated with academic institutions.

Attribution

Any use of the data must credit Europe Elects and give the full poll metadata in any publication, presentation or analysis:

  • pollster
  • commissioner (if applicable)
  • fieldwork dates
  • sample size
  • methodology
Redistribution

You may not redistribute or resell the dataset. By requesting the data, you agree to these terms.

Eligibility & verification

Europe Elects UG may decline requests that don't meet our eligibility or verification criteria. A payment doesn't guarantee delivery if the information given is incomplete or invalid.

Share your work

Please consider sending us any publication that uses the data. If you'd like, we'll consider submissions to contact@europeelects.eu for our social media to help spread the research.
